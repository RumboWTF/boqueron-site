Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Boquerón is a Spanish vocabulary app. Here's what we do with your data.

What we collect

  • Your email — to send you a sign-in code.
  • Words you save and your review history — to make the app work.
  • Nothing else. No name, location, tracking, analytics, or ads.

Voice and camera

Speech in Hablar and voice capture is processed by Apple's Speech Recognition (on-device or via Apple's servers, depending on iOS settings). Camera capture uses Apple Vision, fully on-device. We never receive or store audio or images.

Where your data lives

Supabase (database), with row-level security so only you can read your data. Data is kept while your account is active and removed within 30 days of account deletion.

Third parties

  • Supabase — database and login. https://supabase.com/privacy
  • Anthropic Claude — generates definitions from words you save. We send only the word, never your email. https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Resend — sends login emails. https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple — on-device speech and image processing.

Your rights

Delete your account (Ajustes → "Eliminar cuenta") to remove your data. EU users have the right to access, correct, or delete their data, or to lodge a complaint with their data protection authority.

Children

Boquerón is not directed at children under 13.
